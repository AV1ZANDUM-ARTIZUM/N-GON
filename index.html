Welcome to the Learning Center

Physics describes how objects move, interact, and change. Use the sections below as a quick reference while studying. Try predicting an outcome before checking an equation or running an experiment.

Motion

Velocity describes how quickly position changes. Acceleration describes how velocity changes.

v = Δx / Δt
a = Δv / Δt

Forces

A net force changes an object's motion.

F = m × a

Examples include gravity, friction, tension, and normal force.

Gravity

Near Earth's surface, falling objects accelerate at about 9.8 m/s² when air resistance is ignored.

W = m × g

Energy

Mechanical energy includes kinetic and gravitational potential energy.

KE = ½mv²
PE = mgh

Momentum

Momentum depends on mass and velocity.

p = m × v
