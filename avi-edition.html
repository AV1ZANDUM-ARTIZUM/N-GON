Understanding Forces and Motion

Physics describes how objects move and how forces change that motion. A force can accelerate an object, change its direction, or affect its momentum.

Newton's Second Law

The acceleration of an object depends on the net force applied to it and its mass.

F = ma

Gravity

Gravity is an attractive force between objects with mass. In a simulation, changing the strength of this force can produce very different motion.

Energy

Kinetic energy is associated with motion, while potential energy can be stored because of an object's position.

Research Activity

Use the concepts above to investigate a physics simulation. Observe how changing forces affects motion.
